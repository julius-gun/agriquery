4.2.1 General Inspection

Before commencing any maintenance on the spreading disc assembly, ensure the machine is parked on a level surface with the parking brake engaged. Disconnect the main power take-off (PTO) shaft and lock out the hydraulic system to prevent accidental actuation. Inspect all vane surfaces for signs of excessive wear, cracking, or deformation.

WARNING: Rotating parts. Ensure PTO is disengaged before opening the guard rails.

4.2.2 Vane Replacement & Torque Settings

To replace a vane, remove the retaining bolts on the mounting flange. Clean the mating surfaces thoroughly with a wire brush to remove fertilizer residue and rust. Apply a thin layer of anti-seize compound to the new bolts.

Position the new vane and hand-tighten the lock nuts. Using a calibrated torque wrench, tighten the vane lock nuts in a cross pattern to ensure even pressure distribution. Tighten to 50 Nm. Do not overtighten as this may damage the threaded studs or the vane ceramic coating.

After tightening, verify the clearance between the vane tip and the housing wall. The clearance should be between 2.5mm and 4.0mm. Rotate the disc manually (ensure ignition is OFF) to check for any interference points.
